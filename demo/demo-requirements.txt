Employee Profile Portal — Demo Requirements

1. Access and roles

- Employees must sign in with their company email address and password before they can open any profile screen.
- Employees must be able to reset a forgotten password from the sign-in screen.
- Managers must be able to view the profiles of employees in their team.
- Administrators must be able to deactivate an employee account.

2. Employee profile

- Employees must be able to edit their name, phone number and profile photo.
- The profile form must validate that the phone number contains only digits.
- The profile form must reject profile photos larger than 5 MB.
- Employees must receive a confirmation message after saving their profile.
- The profile photo is optional and an employee may remove it at any time.
- Employees must upload a profile photo before they can save their profile.

3. Manager approvals

- Managers must be able to approve or reject profile changes submitted by their team.
- The dashboard must display a list of pending profile approvals with the employee name and submission date.
- Managers must be able to leave a comment when rejecting a profile change.

4. Notifications and quality

- Employees must be notified by email when their profile change is approved or rejected.
- The interface must meet WCAG AA contrast requirements and support keyboard navigation.
- The application must be responsive and usable on mobile phones and tablets.
- The dashboard should load quickly for teams with up to 500 employees.
- The system must keep an audit record of every approval decision.
- The sign-in screen should look modern and user-friendly.
